What is City Leap?

A 20-Year City Energy
Investment Partnership

Bristol City Leap is a long-term Energy Services Partnership between Bristol City Council and Ameresco — designed to attract private investment into local clean energy infrastructure at scale, accelerating Bristol's journey to carbon neutrality.

Why Bristol?

A City Built for
Climate Leadership

Bristol has long been a pioneer in sustainability — the UK's first European Green Capital in 2015. Building on this legacy, City Leap represents the next chapter: a replicable model for how UK cities can decarbonise their entire energy systems through public-private partnership.

🏆
European Green Capital 2015Bristol's sustainability credentials made it the natural home for a landmark energy partnership.
🌍
Replicable City ModelDesigned from the outset to be scaled and replicated across UK and European cities.
🤝
Community-Centred ApproachPlacing Bristol residents and businesses at the heart of the energy transition.

Climate Action

Aligned to Bristol's
Climate Emergency Declaration

2023 – 2025
Programme Initiation — First tranche of projects mobilised; heat network feasibility and solar portfolio deployment begin.
2025 – 2028
Scale-Up Phase — District heat networks commissioned; EV infrastructure rolled out; 1,000s of home retrofits complete.

2030 – 2043
Sustained Delivery — Continued investment, whole-city decarbonisation, and community energy benefit-sharing.

A Blueprint for UK Cities

From Bristol to
Every UK City

Bristol City Leap is a proof of concept for an entirely new approach to urban energy transition — one that mobilises private finance, builds community wealth, and delivers measurable carbon reductions without burdening public balance sheets.

🗺️
Replicable EverywhereThe City Leap model is designed to be adapted for any UK local authority, regardless of size or geography.
💰
No Net Cost to the Public PursePrivate investment structured to generate returns through energy revenues — freeing up council budgets.
📈
Scalable PipelineAmeresco is actively exploring City Leap-style partnerships with authorities across England, Wales, and Scotland.
